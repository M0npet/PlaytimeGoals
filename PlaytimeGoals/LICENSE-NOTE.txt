PlaytimeGoals is intended as a custom ASF plugin.

The SteamKit2 message-handling approach and playtime-lag reconciliation were designed with reference to:
- ArchiSteamFarm (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/JustArchiNET/ArchiSteamFarm
- ASF-AdvancedIdle (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/cwilliams5/ASF-AdvancedIdle

This source bundle does not include either upstream project's source tree.
